# seihai-asset3d on a real 5.12 (2026-11-27)

Checks that assets converted by `seihai-asset3d` draw on the real Windows
5.12 as seihai-gserver serves them. **Result: PASS after one fix**
(power-of-two textures, below).

Machine: the headless Debian lab VM. The real Windows 5.12 ran under Wine
with Mesa llvmpipe on Xvfb, in a private loopback-only network, against
seihai-gserver main `9ebaec5`. The client's `levels3d/` and
`FILENAMECACHE.txt` were deleted before every run, so every shape and
texture was downloaded in that session. Screenshots, captures and logs
stay in the private lab folder; this page has the derived result.

## World

`seihai-gen terrain W --world lab --size 1x1 --3d --stream --playerworld
--trees 0`, then (servertools `a2ae749`, this branch):

```
seihai-asset3d add W column.glb crate.obj tree.png bigcrate.obj --height 2
seihai-asset3d add W crate.obj --name cratec --colour-only --height 2
seihai-asset3d add W tree.png --name treeblk --solid
seihai-asset3d add W crate.obj --name verylongassetname01 --height 2
```

and each placed with `seihai-edit3d add W NAME NAME.3ds X -56 0 --ground`,
3 tiles apart, seen by a camera looking north and, in a second run, one
looking south. The inputs were made for the lab: a glTF column (+y up)
with a texture of four quadrants (top left red, top right green, bottom
left blue, bottom right white, black border) on every side and a magenta
nose on its front (+z); an OBJ cube with the same quadrants as a JPEG
named `Crate.JPG`; the same cube with a 3000 px PNG; a 44 x 64 RGBA tree
with a transparent background and a yellow mark at its top left.

## Results

| Asset | Drawn | Textured | Looking north (front) | Looking south (back) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| column (glTF) | yes | yes | quadrants the right way round; the nose on the south face, pointing at the camera (the front faces -y) | right way round; nose hidden |
| crate (OBJ + JPEG) | yes | yes | right, top face too | right |
| tree (PNG card) | yes, both sides | yes | background cut out, mark top left; **squeezed** (see below) | mirrored, as expected |
| bigcrate (3000 px source, written at 1024) | yes | yes, not white | right | right |
| cratec (`--colour-only`) | yes | flat colours | the quadrants as colours; the black border as saw teeth along the pieces | right |
| treeblk (`--solid`) | yes | coloured strips | mark top left, no box | mirrored |
| verylongassetname01 | yes | yes | right: a 21-character texture name works | right |

- The client asked for each shape and each texture (by its bare lower-case
  name) once, and the server sent them all; the only not-found answers
  were the client's usual stock files. No client errors, no freeze, no
  crash; each scene was complete at about 4 s and unchanged to 73 s.

## The fix: power-of-two textures

The tree's 44 x 64 texture filled only about 69 % (44/64) of its card,
with the last pixel column smeared over the rest: the 5.12 pads a texture
whose sides are not powers of two and keeps the texture places. The
converter now stretches every texture to the nearest power-of-two sides
(at most 1024), which keeps every texture place right, tiled ones too.

Re-run at `6850cc1` (fresh world, clean cache, looking north): the tree
(now a 32 x 64 texture) and a 48 x 100 test card with a 1-pixel border
(now 64 x 128) both fill their whole card, the border shows on all four
sides, transparent pixels are cut out and no edge column is smeared. The
other assets drew as in the first run. **[lab]**

Not run: the colour-only output on a 4.20r8 (which draws no `.3ds`
textures); the shapes are plain colour materials like every other
servertools shape, which 4.20r8 draws.
